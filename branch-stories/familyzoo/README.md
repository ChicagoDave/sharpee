# Family Zoo in Chord

John Googol's sixteen-step Chord tutorial, mirrored from his IF Hub
(https://johnesco.github.io/ifhub/, hub `familyzoo`) on 2026-10-28.

- `familyzoo/` is the combined "Current" story.
- `familyzoo-v01/` through `familyzoo-v16/` are the tutorial steps, one
  concept each, from a single room (v01) to story states and the closing
  sequence (v16). Step v16 replaced the TypeScript hatch the earlier
  edition used; every step was re-tested by John on Sharpee 5.5.0 /
  Chord 4.0.0.

Each directory holds the `.story`, its `.config.json` (IFID), and the hub's
`WALKTHROUGH.txt`, a sweep of every noun and verb in that step. John's
`.tests` trees are not published on the hub, so none are mirrored here.

The TypeScript editions of the same tutorial, pinned to older platform
versions, live in `tutorials/familyzoo/`.
