- **Tier**: Medium (revised from Small 2026-08-24 after the deep dive — scope grew by world-index and the analyzer diagnostic)
- **Budget**: 150

- **Deliverable** (revised shape, David's ruling 2026-08-24 after the deep dive recorded in the session summary): **compose**, in four pieces. (1) `story-loader`: one **arm-selection function** per (room, direction) — `selectArm(world) → arm | undefined`, declaration order, first line whose condition holds, condition-less line = always-true fallback — with the `exitBlockedKey` closure defined as `selectArm ≠ undefined` and the `exitMessageKey` closure as the selected arm's phrase, so the two keys are two views of one selection and cannot drift (the mergeArms idiom, runtime.ts:799-808, third instance). (3) `chord` analyzer: an unreachable-arm diagnostic (a condition-less blocked line with more lines after it on the same direction) so the order semantics are compile-visible. (4) The semantics sentence for the sharpee.net reference: declaration order; first line whose condition holds supplies the refusal; a condition-less line is the fallback and must be last. Story side: the Northwest Junction's `north is blocked while calm: north-deflected` plus the authored gates-locked lines both wired, pinned in new tree-document cards (calm deflection arm and swept lockout arm). Corpus fact from the dive: no existing story has a multi-line direction, so single-line groups compose to identical closures — zero behavior change outside secret-letter.
