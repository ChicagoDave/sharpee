## Chord, after this plan

A claims block holds `claim` heads only, each with one predicate line and its own `needs` lines. Every predicate form that compiles today still compiles, `<thing> is not in <room>` included: it is now only ever a positive statement that the thing can leave. The lodge's fragment, `test-stories/lodge/lodge.claims.chord`, as Phase 1 leaves it, whole:

```chord
## The lodge's claims: one of each kind the runner can settle, each under the
## rooms, things and verbs it needs. The shared set is the winning line. The
## hand bell is in it on purpose and bears on nothing.

claims
  needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Vault
  needs things: the valve, the hand bell, the strongbox, the deed
  needs verbs: turn, open, take

  claim the story can be won
    the story ends in victory

  claim the deed is in hand
    the player has the deed

  claim the warden has been asked about the vault
    the warden was asked about "the vault"
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall
    needs things: the warden
    needs verbs: ask

  claim the ledger has been studied
    the ledger is studied
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Study
    needs things: the brandy, the warden, the ledger
    needs verbs: take, give, read

  claim the Study is reached
    the player is in the Study
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Study
    needs things: the brandy, the warden
    needs verbs: take, give

  claim the notice has been read
    the notice is read
    needs rooms: the Porch
    needs things: the notice
    needs verbs: read

  claim the valve has been turned
    the valve has been turned
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry
    needs things: the valve
    needs verbs: turn

  claim the strongbox is open
    the strongbox is open
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Vault
    needs things: the valve, the strongbox
    needs verbs: turn, open
end claims
```

`sharpee test test-stories/lodge` then prints `claims: 8 of 8 held (seed 42)` and `4 walks`, where today it prints `claims: 9 of 10 held, 1 violated (seed 42)` and `6 walks` (run 2026-11-14 on the built CLI).

A `never` head is refused at compile:

```chord
claims
  needs rooms: the Landing, the Ticket Hut
  needs things: the ticket
  needs verbs: take

  claim the ticket is in hand
    the player has the ticket

  never the ticket leaves the Ticket Hut
    the ticket is not in the Ticket Hut
end claims
```

One error, `parse.claims-never`, on line 9, the head line; the predicate line under it is consumed and reports nothing:

> `never` is not part of the claims language: a claim states something the story can reach. Remove `never the ticket leaves the Ticket Hut` and the lines under it.

The message carries no decision-record reference. A block whose only head is a `never` reports this error alone, not `parse.claims-empty` as well. `sharpee test` exits 2 on such a fragment and runs no walk. The two `claims.predicate-negated` messages read, for `the kettle is not hot`: "`<claim>`: a claim states what holds — name the state the kettle is in, not one it is not in."; for `the player is not in the Study`: "`<claim>`: a claim states what holds — claim the room the player reaches." The final wording of all three is settled at the phase's edit; the codes and what each message says are fixed here.

The grammar rule, `packages/chord/chord.ebnf`, after Phase 1 (notes abbreviated):

```
claims-block = "claims" NL
                 >>> { needs-line } claim-decl { claim-decl }
               "end" "claims" NL ;   (* parse.claims-empty: no claim; parse.claims-line: a line the
                                        block does not know; a line headed `never` is
                                        parse.claims-never — a claim states what the story can reach *)
claim-decl   = "claim" rest-of-line NL
                 >>> claim-predicate { needs-line } ;
```
