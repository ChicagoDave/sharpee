# Session Plan: Research program — the conversational side of NPC agency

**Created**: 2026-08-16
**Plan Status**: ABANDONED (2026-08-16 — David: the work starts as an ADR after merging the two prior-art documents, not as a phased research plan; the References-consulted survey work in this file carries into `conversation-prior-art.md` and the ADR)
**Overall scope**: A research program, not an implementation plan. ADR-310/318 just shipped the NPC's *interior* (temperament, mood, disposition, goals, influence, propagation, principles, honor, conscience, lie ledger, arbitration). This program researches the *conversational* side sitting on top of it — what complex, lifelike dialogue looks like, and what Sharpee/Chord need to support it — and produces a synthesis document ready to seed a brainstorm session and, eventually, one or more ADRs. No implementation phases; no story prose is authored as content (illustrative snippets only, clearly marked).
**Bounded contexts touched**: Character (interior model, `@sharpee/character`), Conversation (topic dispatch + dialogue selection, `character/src/conversation/`, ADR-239), Chord (authoring surface for both), Client/Reflections UX (multi-actor presentation). No code changes — research documents only, written into these contexts' vocabulary.
**Key domain language**: quip/phrase (ADR-310 D15's terminology note), topic table, dialogue-selector socket, reveal arbitration, ConvNode (TADS 3 term used comparatively), disposition/mood-shaped selection, NPC-initiated conversation.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Internal audit — what the shipped conversation surface supports and where it strains
- **Tier**: Medium
- **Budget**: 250
- **Domain focus**: Conversation (topic dispatch + dialogue selection), evidenced against the Character interior model
- **Entry state**: ADR-310/318 acceptance-audit CLOSED (verified); thealderman is the six-suspect interrogation story exercising the shipped surface end to end.
- **Deliverable**: `docs/work/npc-agency-conversation-research/01-internal-audit.md` — a walk of the actual conversation path (`define topics for …` → `character/src/conversation/{selector,dialogue-extension,acl,claims,constraint-evaluator,topic-registry}.ts` → stdlib ASK/TELL/SAY/TALK TO actions) against concrete thealderman scenes, naming: (a) what the topic-table + specificity + arbitration stack can express today (voice selection by specificity, character-scoped-beats-story-scoped, one-active-voice-wins-outright per D16, reveal arbitration gating confided topics, lie mint/pin through topic dispatch); (b) where it strains — cite the D15 finding that the dialogue-selector socket has zero production registrants (Chord runs through topic dispatch only), the four issue #268 expressiveness gaps, and any additional strain the thealderman transcripts/acceptance-audit surfaced (e.g. the binary match/no-match nature of topic rows vs. graded response selection). Cross-check every finding against the design-pattern catalog's `Conversation`/`NPC` categories — which CONV-*/NPC-* patterns are implemented, partially implemented, or absent.
- **Exit state**: A concrete, evidence-cited inventory of "what exists" and "where it strains" that Phases 3 and 4 design against, instead of re-discovering.

### Phase 3: The interior-to-dialogue seam — from gating to shaping
- **Tier**: Medium
- **Budget**: 250
- **Domain focus**: Character ↔ Conversation seam — how mood, temperament, disposition, goals, and the lie ledger should shape *what* is said and *how*, not only gate *whether*
- **Entry state**: Phase 1's strain inventory (dialogue-selector socket unbuilt; topic dispatch is match/no-match) and Phase 2's axis-organized prior art (particularly Comme il Faut's social-physics continuous scoring and Façade's beat-driven tonal shaping, both prior-art inputs already on file).
- **Deliverable**: `docs/work/npc-agency-conversation-research/03-interior-to-dialogue-seam.md` — a design-options memo (not a decision) laying out candidate mechanisms for interior state to shape phrasing/tone/subtext, not merely gate row visibility: e.g. graded selection within a matched topic row (mood-conditioned phrasing variants), the dialogue-selector socket D15 already named but left unbuilt, and how the four issue #268 expressiveness gaps (propagation coloring, goal interruption, influence lingering, resist-excepts) bear on "shaping" specifically vs. "gating." Each candidate mechanism is evaluated against thealderman evidence (Phase 1) and against at least one external precedent (Phase 2) for cost/benefit, not picked.
- **Exit state**: A set of named, evidence-grounded design options for the shaping seam, with tradeoffs stated but no decision made — decisions belong to the brainstorm/ADR stage.
- **Status**: ABANDONED (2026-08-16 — never started)

### Phase 4: Multi-party and NPC-initiated conversation
- **Tier**: Medium
- **Budget**: 250
- **Domain focus**: NPC agency in conversation — NPCs starting, steering, exiting conversations; multi-party scenes
- **Entry state**: Phase 1's inventory (reveal arbitration's witnessing/propagation machinery, ADR-318 AC4, is multi-NPC-aware today at the fact-propagation level, not at the live-conversation level); Phase 2's Façade/multi-party findings; the design-pattern catalog's CONV-006 (Multi-Party Conversation), CONV-011 (Eavesdropping), NPC-027 (NPC Initiative).
- **Deliverable**: `docs/work/npc-agency-conversation-research/04-multi-party-npc-initiated.md` — examines what "NPC agency" means on the conversational side specifically: NPC-initiated dialogue (an NPC opening a conversation unprompted, per goal pursuit's suspend-during-conversation lifecycle rule from ADR-310 D16), NPC-steered conversation (redirecting, refusing to answer, changing subject — distinct from the already-shipped refuse-to-answer gating), and multi-party scenes where more than one NPC (or NPC+player) participate. Pulls `reflections-ux.md`'s concrete requirements (three actors, rotating PC, chat-bubble alignment flips) as a forcing function — treat its UX findings as live requirements, its client-architecture discussion (Zifmia-era) as historical context only. Names what of this the shipped witnessing/propagation machinery already answers vs. what is net-new.
- **Exit state**: A clear map of "what multi-party/NPC-initiated conversation needs" against "what the interior model already provides for free," with the gap named.
- **Status**: ABANDONED (2026-08-16 — never started)
