# Wumpus Caves — Phase 2 probes (2026-10-21)

Each probe is a piped `./sharpee play branch-stories/wumpus` run (unseeded; the
tree pins seed 42 later). The story as written at Phase 1 (coracle `sight: 3`)
unless a probe says otherwise.

## P1 — pit death on arrival

Commands: `enter coracle`, `set course for the Bottomless Pit`, `wait` ×3.

Result: turn 2 of the course printed "The coracle arrives at the Bottomless
Pit."; the kill (`on every turn while the coracle is at the Bottomless Pit`)
fired on the **next** turn with the `pit-death` text, and the engine stopped.

Reading: the vehicle's arrival happens inside the turn's machine step, after
the story's every-turn rules have been read for that turn, so `is at` sees
the arrival one turn late. One turn of lag is acceptable for the hunt (the
classic game also reports the fall as the result of the move). `deadly:` on
the room would give the same one-command lag. Keeping `is at`.

## P2 — the bat roost relocates the coracle

Commands: `enter coracle`, `set course for the Bat Roost`, `wait` ×9, then
`steer east`, `wait`, `look`.

Result: arrival at the Bat Roost; next turn the `bats-carry` phrase and a
`select randomly` arm's `move the coracle to <room>` ran. Two runs landed in
two different rooms (the Dripping Gallery, the Echo Chamber). From the new
room `steer east` put the coracle under way again; the following `look`
described the Wumpus Caves with sighting rows measured from the new room's
cell (the Echo Chamber "in the distance, to the west").

Reading: a runtime `move` of an open vehicle onto a landmark room clears its
position and the vehicle departs from that landmark's coordinates, as the
ADR's D5 says a landmark departure does. No platform gap.

## P3 — scent, out-of-sight shot, eaten

Commands: `enter coracle`, `set course for the Echo Chamber`, `wait` ×4,
`look`, `shoot wumpus`, `steer east`.

Result: in the Echo Chamber (a landmark room with no `sight:`), `shoot
wumpus` was refused with "You can't see any such thing." — sight is scope,
and a landmark room without `sight:` sees nothing of the expanse. `steer
east` took one step; the same turn printed the scent (`when the Wumpus comes
into sight`) **and** the eaten death.

Reading: with `sight: 3`, "close by" is within 1 unit, so a Wumpus that has
drifted toward the Echo Chamber comes into sight and is already close by in
one step. The hunt needs a window: the coracle's sight goes to 6 (horizon
within 6, in the distance within 4, close by within 2), the shot hits only
when the Wumpus is **in the distance**, and the Wumpus eats when **close
by**. Story change, no platform gap.

## P4 — room contents list in the expanse

Commands: `enter coracle`, `steer north`, `look` (Wumpus about 7 units off).

Result: "You can see a coracle here." — the Wumpus was not listed. In P2's
`look`, with the Wumpus on the horizon, the list read "You can see a coracle
and a Wumpus here." beside its sighting row.

Reading: the contents list of the expanse room follows sight, as scope does;
a sighted floating thing is both listed and given a band row. Consistent, no
gap. Distance is Euclidean: from (2,3) the Bottomless Pit at (4,5) was "on
the horizon" at sight 3 (2.83 units); from (2,4) it was "in the distance"
(2.24 units).

## P5 — `stop coracle` under `drive: player`

Result: "The coracle isn't something you can switch off." — `stop <vehicle>`
reached the switching-off action, not the vehicle's stop.

Reading: not a gap. `stop <vehicle>` is the story's statement (ADR-366 D5f);
the player's word is `heave to` (steering's patterns in
`packages/lang-en-us/src/actions/steering.ts`: `steer [direction]`, `heave
to`). The tree uses `heave to`.

## P6 — a landmark room's outlook is lost aboard the vehicle (PLATFORM GAP)

Seed 42, probe cards through `./sharpee test --json --capture-output` with
`skip: true` (the Echo Chamber now carries `sight: 6`): arrive at the Echo
Chamber aboard → `look` lists no sightings; `exit` → `look` lists "The
Bottomless Pit, in the distance, to the northwest. The Landing, in the
distance, to the west. The Wumpus, on the horizon, to the east. The Bone
Hollow, on the horizon, to the northeast."; `enter coracle` → `look` lists
none again; `shoot wumpus` aboard is "You can't see any such thing."

Cause (read): `packages/world-model/src/traits/expanse/sightBehavior.ts`
`lookoutOf` — the aboard-an-open-vehicle branch returns `undefined` when the
vehicle has no position (it is at a landmark, D5) instead of falling through
to the room branch. Filed as a GitHub issue (see the session summary for the
number); no `packages/` change made. Story-level: the hunter leaves the
coracle to look out from a chamber, which is also how the tree reads a
chamber's outlook.

## P8 — `the player's arrows` fails at runtime (PLATFORM GAP)

With `counter arrows starts 5` on the Hunter, the shooting action's `refuse
when the player's arrows is 0`, `lower the player's arrows by 1` and `lose
out-of-arrows when the player's arrows is 0` compile clean and fail in play:
"One of the story's rules failed here: Field `arrows` is not supported
here." Renamed to `the Hunter's arrows`, all three work: five shots at the
Bottomless Pit from the shore print `arrow-misses` five times and the fifth
prints `out-of-arrows` and ends the story. Filed as a GitHub issue; the
story keeps the name.

## P7 — the seed-42 hunt

Drift is a function of the turn number alone at seed 42 (the only random
consumer), so the Wumpus's cell at turn *n* is the same in every line.

Win (9 probe cards): `enter coracle`, `steer east`, `wait` ×3 (turn 5: the
scent, "The coracle arrives at the Echo Chamber."), `steer east`, `look`
("The Wumpus, in the distance, to the east."), `shoot wumpus` → "The arrow
goes home. The Wumpus rears once out of the water, enormous, and goes down
for good. The caves are quiet." and the engine stops.

Edge: `enter coracle`, `steer west`, `look` (the Landing close by to the
east), `wait` → "The west wall of the cavern, running with water. Nothing
passes it."; `steer west` → the same line; `steer east` → "You steer the
coracle east." D8 as written.

Bats at seed 42: `set course for the Bat Roost` arrives on the eighth wait
(the scent on the third); the next turn prints `bats-carry` and the `select
randomly` arm lands the coracle in the Bone Hollow.

Eaten (P3's run 2, before the shooting rewrite): the step that brought the
Wumpus within 2 units printed `eaten` the same turn, before any command
could be typed — the hunt's rule as designed. Phase 3 derives its exact
seed-42 line.
