"Every character" stays the eventual intent; the rest are added as Phases 7 and 8 reach their conversations. **Mechanism clarified 2026-08-22 (David): an idiom layer, not per-character asides.** The Vedd are a mystical group of women with real power, founded on **the essence of truth — peeling away the layers of life to reveal a thing's true nature** — mysterious, well respected, and believed lost from the world for centuries. That theology **is the remake's own premise** (Jack's true nature under a presented surface; §3c's talent as seeing past the physical; the coda as the literal peeling-away), which turns the idiom layer from flavour into foreshadowing carried in diction. Their long absence is why the idioms survive as fossils: the speech outlived the order. They are prominent enough that idioms about them are woven through the whole population's speech — the technique David names is *The Shadow in the Cathedral*'s machinery sayings. So the seeding is a **register applied across dialogue as diction**, not a set of authored disclosures, and the four are where that register is first fixed rather than four characters who uniquely know something. Two consequences: **the world's properties are axiomatic** (David, 2026-08-22) — neither the Vedd nor the talent is ever explained, which forecloses both magic mechanics *and* exposition as work items, so no ADR, trait, or behavior models the Vedd, no lore/cosmology/origin document is written, and the coda is authored as a scene rather than built or justified as a system; and the talent/Vedd collision this plan briefly carried as open is **dissolved**, since an idiom implies nothing about the speaker's perception (vision.md §2).
