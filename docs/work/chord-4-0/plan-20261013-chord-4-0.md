## Sequencing rationale
- Cheapest and lowest-risk implementation first (phases 1, 4-5); the two highest-uncertainty questions, the vehicle and lock-from-play, get their ADR discussion up front (phase 2) because that costs conversation, not code, and their answers decide phases 11-17.
- Gerund list (phase 3) precedes the new clause shapes (7-8) so the two-noun clauses are designed against the final clause head.
- Independent after phase 2/3: syntax families 4-9 can run in any order; they share only the manifest/catalog files. The vehicle chain 10-13 is sequential (enforcement, then words, then models, then conveyances). Claims chain 14-15 is independent of everything but phase 2 and UPPS having rooms to lock. Exit passages and push-between-rooms ride the vehicle's `going` changes, so they follow phase 10.
- Phase count is higher than usual because each syntax family is its own ADR plus a platform change; families, not rows, are the unit.
