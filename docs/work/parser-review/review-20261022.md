# Parser and Chord grammar seam — expert review

**Date**: 2026-10-22, session 51f88b, on `chord-4-0`.
**Scope**: the English command parser (`packages/parser-en-us`), its contracts (`packages/if-domain/src/grammar`, `vocabulary-contracts`, `parser-contracts`), the noun-resolution handoff (`packages/stdlib/src/validation`, `stdlib/src/helpers/multi-object-handler.ts`), and the seam to Chord (`packages/chord` pattern parsing, analysis and IR; `packages/story-loader` rule registration and dispatch; the standard grammar `parser-en-us/grammar/standard-en-us.story` and `tools/repokit/src/commands/grammar.ts`).
**Method**: three independent read-only reviews (the matcher; noun resolution; the Chord ↔ parser seam), none given the others' or this session's conclusions, each required to cite file:line and to probe behavior where it could. Their findings were merged here, deduplicated across reviewers, and spot-checked against the code by this session. Each finding says how it was confirmed: **[checked]** re-read or re-run by this session; **[probe]** a reviewer ran a scratch script or story through the real parser or the CLI bundle (scripts and stories in the session scratchpad, `…/scratchpad/probe/`); **[read]** from the cited lines only.

## Verdict

The parser is a first-fit pattern matcher that decides each slot's extent once, from the next pattern token, with no search; it ranks matches by a "confidence" that is really a count of skipped optional words, above the story tier that is meant to win; and it decides grammatical roles (verb, direct object, preposition, direction) by heuristics over vocabulary tags and pattern-string substrings rather than from the rule that matched. On the Chord side, a structured IR pattern is flattened to an English-parser string and re-split in three places that each read brackets differently, and the loader's dispatch binds only one slot per action.

These are not independent bugs. Six root causes produce almost every defect below, and patching them one at a time would leave the causes in place. The defects are real today: `give the troll the sword` seats "the" as the recipient in every story; a Chord action with two nouns never binds the second; a passage whose command holds a compass word walks the compass way.

## Root causes

1. **No search over slot boundaries.** A slot ends at the first token equal to the next pattern element; consecutive slots split at one word; an optional element is tried once. Nothing backtracks or scores alternatives (`entity-slot-consumer.ts:264-333`, `english-grammar-engine.ts:270-318`).
2. **A stringly-typed seam.** `IRPatternPart[]` is rendered to pattern text (`loader.ts:3198`), then split again by the pattern compiler, by `fullPattern`'s slot scan (`if-domain/src/grammar/grammar-engine.ts:449-453`) and by the parser's role map (`english-parser.ts:816-831`), each differently; the renderer itself exists four times (loader, analyzer removal check `analyzer.ts:1943`, repokit `grammar.ts:87`, passage expansion `parser.ts:350`).
3. **Roles by heuristic, not by rule.** Verb = leading VERB-tagged words (`english-parser.ts:786-801`); preposition = first PREPOSITION-tagged word (`:1026-1033`); direct object = first slot, except pattern substrings `give :recipient :item`, `show …`, `:item from :container` (`:966-1006`); going's direction = first DIRECTION-tagged token anywhere in the input (`:1038-1056`); Chord's dispatch noun = first entity slot across all of an action's patterns (`chord/src/ir.ts:1450`, read by `dispatch-verbs.ts:314`), with every other slot unbound.
4. **Ranking by a vestigial confidence above tier.** Every slot consumer returns 1.0 or no match; `experimentalConfidence` is set by nothing; so confidence is 0.9 per skipped optional word, ranked before tier (`english-grammar-engine.ts:113-123`, copied at `english-parser.ts:408-416`).
5. **Three noun matchers that disagree.** The parse-time scope gate (`grammar-scope-resolver.ts:299-337`, exact or substring), the validator (`command-validator.ts:735-803`, word tiers over name, aliases and adjectives) and the list/"all but" expander (`multi-object-handler.ts:185-192`, head word only).
6. **Language and state in the wrong homes.** Articles are listed in six places across four packages; English action ids and slot names are special-cased in the parser; the pronoun manager and the vocabulary registry are process-global.

Severity: **DEFECT** wrong today, with an input; **DESIGN** a wrong abstraction that holds today and will bite; **CLEANUP** dead, constant or duplicated.

## What is sound

- Story rules are iterated first in stable definition order; the rule gate (`when`) removes a gated-off rule entirely (`english-grammar-engine.ts:76-86`).
- `literalSpecificity` is a real, load-bearing ranking term (`push :target north` over `push :target`; `take off :item` over `take :item`).
- The IR pattern form (`IRPatternPart`, kind plus optional) is structured and language-neutral up to the loader.
- Splitting slot types into consumers behind a registry is the right extension point; the search in root cause 1 belongs inside it.
- Topic slots are verbatim, never scope-rejected, entity-first with a text fallback (ADR-231 D4).
- Scope bases call the world's real visibility, reachability and carried surfaces and fail closed (ADR-273).
- The validator's own ranking (tier, matched words, score) is principled, and it alone surfaces ambiguity, with entity ids.
- `removeRules` by (action, pattern, tier) is a clean primitive; alteration targets and removal shapes are checked at compile time against the manifest.
- The standard grammar round trip (one compile feeding `grammar.ts` and the manifest, with a freshness check) and grammar-file mode's gates are sound.
- `[the]` vs `[the target]` is unambiguous; a pattern's first word is never an article.

## Recommendation

Fix the six causes, not the forty symptoms. In order:

1. **Ship-breaking, small, now** (each a few lines, each with a real-path test): delete the going direction scan and let a rule's direction semantics stand (C3); bind every entity slot at dispatch by the matched rule's slot names (C1, with C2's seat below); make `→` a compile error until it is real (B2); lowercase pattern words at lowering (B3); count phrase placeholders in `slot-unused`, skip it in grammar-file mode, write `furl [the] sails` (G1–G3); remove the `throw` log.
2. **One structured seam** (root cause 2): a builder entry that takes pattern parts, not text; one renderer and directions expander in chord used by the loader, the analyzer and repokit; slots, seats and constraints read from the compiled pattern, never by re-splitting strings. This fixes B1 and makes B5–B7 trivial.
3. **Roles declared, not guessed** (root cause 3): each rule carries which slot is the direct object, which the indirect, which the instrument and which a topic or text (a builder method, emitted by Chord per pattern, with a default rule stated once); the parser seats by it, failure messages read it, dispatch binds by slot name; the verb and preposition come from the literals the rule matched. Removes the substring tests and the action-id special case.
4. **Ranking** (root cause 4): drop confidence; rank tier, then literal specificity, then definition order; supersede ADR-268 D2's confidence term; consider an analyzer warning for D3.
5. **A matcher that searches** (root cause 1): slot consumers return candidate end points; the engine searches them (bounded) and scores each complete parse by how well its nouns resolve, through the one noun matcher of step 6. This is the TADS 3 shape at Sharpee's scale.
6. **One noun matcher** (root cause 5): the validator's word-tier matcher, extracted to one home, called by the parse-time gate (filtered by scope base), by list and `all but` expansion, and by the search's scoring; pronouns pass the same gate; determiners and articles split once by the English parser and nowhere else.
7. **Language and state** (root cause 6): one article, determiner, self-word and direction table per language, owned by the language; the pronoun manager and vocabulary registry per parser instance.

Steps 2–6 are platform changes that each need a decision record: step 4 supersedes ADR-268 D2; steps 2 and 3 change the if-domain grammar contract and Chord's lowering; step 5 replaces the matcher's core. They are coupled (the search in 5 needs the seats of 3 and the matcher of 6), so they want one plan, sequenced 2 → 3 → 4 → 6 → 5 → 7, with Dungeo's chain and every Chord tree as the regression floor at each step.
