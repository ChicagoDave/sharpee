## Coupling with step 3 (stated, not absorbed)
Step 3 adds `seat` (direct, indirect, instrument, topic, text) to a rule's slots, emitted by Chord per pattern, and removes the substring seat tests. Step 2 must therefore leave: (1) a slot in the compiled pattern as a record (`name`, `optional`, `greedy`, `slotType`, position among slots) that a `seat` field can join without reshaping; (2) the builder entry taking a rule spec object, so `seat` becomes one more field on it rather than a second entry point; (3) the parser's role map reading that record. Step 2 does **not** decide how a seat is declared, what the default rule is, or what Chord's syntax for it is. The one overlap to watch: B1's fix gives an optional slot a position among slots, which is the *positional default* (first slot is direct object). Step 2 keeps exactly today's positional rule (an optional slot counts like a required one) and leaves "should it?" to step 3.
