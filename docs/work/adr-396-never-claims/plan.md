## Chord, as this plan builds it

The claims fragment's grammar changes in Phase 3, and two meanings change in Phases 4 and 5. Lines inside a claim are written in one order, which the fragment's canonical gate enforces and `renderClaimBlock` writes: the predicate line, then `excluding things:`, then `needs rooms:`, `needs things:`, `needs verbs:`, each at most once. A predicate line may join facts with `and`. Comments stay at the top level.

The forms, against `test-stories/lodge`:

```chord
## Every form this plan adds, written against the lodge. Not a committed file.

claims
  needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Vault
  needs things: the valve, the hand bell, the strongbox, the deed
  needs verbs: turn, open, take

  never the strongbox leaves the Vault
    the strongbox is not in the Vault

  never the hand bell leaves the Pantry
    the hand bell is not in the Pantry
    needs verbs: take

  claim the Study is reached
    the player is in the Study
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Study
    needs things: the brandy, the warden
    needs verbs: take, give

  never the Study is reached without the brandy
    the player is in the Study
    excluding things: the brandy
    needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Study
    needs things: the hand bell, the warden
    needs verbs: take, give

  never the Study is reached before the warden has the brandy
    the player is in the Study and the warden does not have the brandy

  claim the deed is in hand with the strongbox open
    the player has the deed and the strongbox is open
end claims
```

What each one does once Phase 5 is built:

- **the strongbox leaves the Vault** writes no needs, so it takes nothing from the block and no walk runs for it (D2a). It is watched after every card of every tree line and at every state of every claims walk. Its row reads NOT VIOLATED, settled by the watch, with how many lines and walks watched it.
- **the hand bell leaves the Pantry** writes one needs line, which asks for a search: it is walked under its own verbs and the block's rooms and things, and also watched everywhere.
- **the Study is reached** is the positive twin. Its needs name the brandy, so it shows the goal is reachable with the thing the necessity does without (D3a).
- **the Study is reached without the brandy** is a necessity. Its walk types no command naming the brandy. On a played line it is watched until a command resolves to the brandy; inside a walk it is watched only when that walk's set does not contain the brandy (D2). If no twin names the brandy, or the twin is not held, its line adds that no claim shows the goal is reachable with the brandy.
- **the Study is reached before the warden has the brandy** is an ordering. It needs no exclusion, so it is watched on every turn of every run with no cut-off.
- **the deed is in hand with the strongbox open** is a positive claim with two facts. It holds where both do (D3b).

Each of these is refused at compile, naming the claim, and no walk runs:

```chord
claims
  needs rooms: the Porch, the Hall, the Pantry, the Study
  needs things: the brandy, the warden
  needs verbs: take, give

  claim the Study is reached without the bell
    the player is in the Study
    excluding things: the hand bell

  never the Study is reached without the brandy
    the player is in the Study
    excluding things: the brandy
    needs things: the brandy, the warden

  never the Study is reached without the Hall
    the player is in the Study
    excluding things: the Hall

  never the Study is reached without help
    the player is in the Study
    excluding: the brandy

  never the player is anywhere but here
    the player is in the Study or the player is in the Vault
end claims
```

In order: `excluding things:` on a positive claim (`claims.excluding-positive`); an excluded thing in the set the claim is searched under (`claims.excluding-needed`); a room or the player excluded (`claims.excluding-thing-kind`; an unknown name stays `claims.unknown-entity`); a bare `excluding:`, and likewise `excluding rooms:` or `excluding verbs:` (`parse.claim-excluding`, whose message says only things may be excluded); a part that is none of the forms (`claims.predicate-unsupported`, unchanged, listing the forms with the negated possession added). Error messages carry no decision-record references.
