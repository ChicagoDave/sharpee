Breaking, the reason it is a major

  • Every create block names its kind, and the kind leads the block: a thing, a room, a container, openable. A block without a kind, or with a trait before it, stops compiling. The inline form create the Potting Shed, a room is admitted.
  • Hatches are gone. define text … from, define action … from and define chain … from are parse errors with fix-its. Every Chord story is pure IR with no TypeScript reach-through.
  • A region declares its rooms instead of nesting them: a rooms group on the region, in <region> on a room. containing and nested regions stop compiling. Regions get an exit table.
  • A vehicle's passage: line becomes a passage … end passage block, and a flight's trip time is pace:.
  • with max items N and with max weight N are gone. holds <n> is the one spelling of capacity.
  • use state-machines is an error. define machine is core and needs no use line.

The kind leads, in the long form and the inline form:

create the watering can
  a thing
  aka can, tin can, sprinkler
  in the Potting Shed

  A dented tin watering can with a long spout.

create the Potting Shed, a room

  Clay pots in stacks, and a smell of damp earth.

A region declares its rooms:

create the Grounds
  a region
  landing the Drive

  after the player entering

  end after

create the Maze
  a region

      Maze

    You are in a maze of twisty little passages, all alike.

  • Blocks fold: create <name>, <composition> and exits: / exits, one-way: lines.

Vehicles and movement

  • The vehicle is a kind with a movement model: line, streets, flight, compass, open and none. admits, to outside, boarding, the driving statements and predicates.
  • use sailing: bearings in degrees, the naval commands, wind and sails.
  • use expanses: open movement on coordinates, landmarks, drift, edges, start <vehicle> heading <direction>.
  • Passages and hidden exits: a passage is a block with its own grammar and while, blocked and deadly lines. An exit exists while a condition holds. A thing's own pushing block moves it between rooms. [the] is optional in a pattern.
  • One rule, one home: traversal and visibility answer the walking player, a vehicle, a pushed thing and an NPC alike.

Things, containers, physical state

  • Containers say what they show and hold: transparent, holds <n>. in and out are exit directions.
  • attached to / detachable; breakable, fragile, starts broken, broken as a state, after the player breaking. Tying is a stock action.
  • Object-side adjectives: lowering and raising take the clause body as their implementation. flammable [with the <tool>] gates burning. settable makes a thing's reversible states a dial, with a setting head that takes to <value>.
  • Lamps: light, put out, light-source with, starts lit, powered by. dark is reserved as a state name off rooms.

Verbs and conversation

  • Stock verbs with no object side: kissing, waving, jumping, saying yes / no / sorry, looking under, emptying.
  • Two-noun clauses: a head names its counterpart, as in on the player throwing at the sorting machine.
  • ask <person> for <thing> is a request. A readable thing carries define topics and answers consult. costs <n> <currency> prices a thing and gates buying.
  • A define action answers its topic slot with about rows, the same row form define topics uses.

State machines

  • define machine is core: states, on enter, when transitions, a terminal state, and roles bound to things in the story.
define machine ferry
  role capstan is the capstan
  starts moored

  state moored
    when turning the capstan: casting-off

  state casting-off
    on enter
      phrase rope-pays-out
    end on
    when signaled: underway

  state underway, terminal
    on enter
      phrase ferry-underway
    end on
end machine

Testing

  • A reached state can be locked as a claim: sharpee claim <line> "<fact>" proposes the necessary set from the played line, writes the claim and pins the card. A broken lock is a failed card named in the claim's words.
  • Claims under a necessary set prune the explorer to the winning path; an inert needs is a diagnostic.
  • The test tree is segmented on disk as <story>.tests/ with a manifest and stable card ids.
  • Derived rule tests: arrange(), END STATE cards prove endings, sharpee test reports branches, endings and rooms.
  • The IDE's Testing tab keeps stale results, names each failed card's cause, and walks failures one stop at a time.
claims
  needs rooms: the Iron Gates, the Gravel Drive, the Fountain Court, the Boiler Shed, the Entrance Hall, the Kitchen, the Greenhouse, the Folly Hill, the Folly
  needs things: Tobias, the stopcock, the primer plunger, the boiler, the garden shears, the sherry bottle, Mrs Kettle, the vine, the silver locket, the folly door, the fuse, the deed box, the deed
  needs verbs: ask, turn, push, turn on, take, give, prune, open, cut

  claim the story can be won
    the story ends in victory

  claim Tobias has been asked about the folly
    Tobias was asked about "the folly"
    needs rooms: the Iron Gates, the Gravel Drive
    needs things: Tobias
    needs verbs: ask

  never the deed box leaves the Folly
    the deed box is not in the Folly
    needs rooms: the Iron Gates, the Gravel Drive, the Fountain Court, the Boiler Shed, the Greenhouse, the Folly Hill, the Folly
    needs things: Tobias, the stopcock, the primer plunger, the boiler, the folly door, the deed box
    needs verbs: ask, turn, push, turn on, open, take
end claims

The same gesture from the command line. The first argument is a line of the test tree, named by its segment id (weefpbmr here, from Fernhill's tree) or by the first command typed on it; --at is the turn as you count them. Without a fact, the command lists what is true at the line's last turn. With one, --propose prints the claim block it would write, and without --propose it writes the claim and pins the card.

sharpee claim weefpbmr
sharpee claim weefpbmr "the deed box is open" --at 22 --propose
sharpee claim weefpbmr "the deed box is open" --at 22

What the player notices from the parser review

  • give the old man the sword, put jack in the box in chest and take salt and pepper shaker read right. and and a comma are list readings. unlock door with asks for the key.
  • A missing object is asked for by its seat, and a gate failure names the phrase and the reason.
  • your lantern is the player's own in the second-person voice. Parse-failure sentences belong to the language package.
