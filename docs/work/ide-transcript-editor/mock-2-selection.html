Select the words that matter

ADR-300 D4. This mock is not a layout — it is one interaction, shown in three states.

The corpus says [OK: contains "…"] is 89.8 % of every assertion authors write (2822 of 3144) — and the contains family is 92.6 %. ADR-299's exporter could only emit verbatim [OK] — 5 uses in the whole repository. So the primary gesture has to produce a fragment, and the natural way to name a fragment is to select it.

Drag across the actual output. The editor writes the assertion. Accept the whole response instead and you get [OK] + a literal block. The rare form stays reachable; the common one is one drag.

State 1 — the turn has run. Nothing is asserted yet.
> north
Gravel Drive The drive curves between bare lime trees, gravel crunching underfoot. The house looms ahead to the north; the gates are back south. You can see Tobias here.
skip nothing asserted yet
State 2 — the author drags across two words.
> north
Gravel Drive The drive curves between bare lime trees, gravel crunching underfoot. The house looms ahead to the north; the gates are back south. You can see Tobias here.
contains "Gravel Drive"
The chip previews the assertion before it is written. "not contains" flips the same selection into the negative form — 87 uses, and the second most common thing authors want.
State 3 — asserted, and the turn is green.
> north
Gravel Drive The drive curves between bare lime trees, gravel crunching underfoot. The house looms ahead to the north; the gates are back south. You can see Tobias here.
contains "Gravel Drive"
The highlight persists: it shows which part of this response the test actually pins. Everything unmarked is free to change without breaking anything — which is the whole reason authors reach for contains over exact match.
The drift case — prose reworded; ADR-282 D2 / ADR-300 D7.
> take the tarnished key
You pick up the tarnished key.
contains "You take the tarnished key" — failed
Rebless.swift already refuses to silently widen a contains fragment into the whole new response — that would weaken a claim the author made on purpose. So the refusal becomes two explicit buttons instead of one automatic rewrite. This is the affordance ADR-282 D2 always implied and never had a home for.
Why this matters more than the layout: whichever of mocks 1, 3, 4 or 5 wins, this gesture is the thing that makes the tool worth building. It is the one capability the skein could not have at any level of polish, because "which fragment do I care about" is a judgment it had nowhere to put.
