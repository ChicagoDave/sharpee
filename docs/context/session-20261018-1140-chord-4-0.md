## Key Decisions
- The tick on the turn a vehicle is started counts toward its pace: `pace: 1` moves the same turn as the steer.
- "steer and go are one action" (D5c) is a parsed-command transformer, not a delegation inside either action, so neither duplicates the other's phases and interceptors fire on the owning action.
- `set course for` takes free text (a topic slot) because destination rooms are never in scope.
- `vehicle-arrived` is reported when the player is aboard or in the room reached; `vehicle-stopped` only when aboard; a flight departure is silent (the author's `becomes under way` sequence narrates the fold).
- The machine's reports live under going's message namespace (`going-vehicle-arrived`), as the vehicle refusals already do, keeping the alias bijection and `override message`.
- Report params are entity names, not noun phrases: plugin-state-machine cannot reach stdlib's `nounPhraseFor`. Flagged, not fixed.
