### The mood/state detour → ADR-318
- David challenged the Q3 answer directly ("not convinced you've thought through what mood and state are"), prompting a code-level read: Chord entity states are forward-ratcheted, scoreable plot facts (`ir.ts:229`); mood is valence/arousal coordinates (`MOOD_AXES`) with decay, rendered as a tone suffix (`acl.ts:85`, `selectMoodVariant`).
- David then reframed the question upward: what *are* the elements of dialogue, and how do they weave through human nature — mood is one, alongside character, honor, ethics, conscience. A seven-layer ontology of dialogue was laid out in conversation; layers 5 (normative), 6 (commitment), 7 (arbitration) were identified as missing from ADR-310 entirely.
- David set the design brief: stretch toward Versu's depth of interiority while keeping authoring easy. Four working rules: words-never-rules (author writes prose, runtime never invents dialogue), depth is opt-in via the D4/D5 ladder, emergence speaks only through authored mouths (D12 as the safety rail), and the runtime boils the pot — the author writes the boiling over.
- David asked for an instrument to iterate the missing layers against that brief: `docs/work/adr-310/experiments/method.md` — a scenario corpus (B1-B7 imported from prior art, N1-N3 reserved as thealderman slots, David's specific picks still pending) scored on a Depth/Cost/Predictability/Legibility RED/YELLOW/GREEN rubric via a hand-trace loop.
- Five experiments run, all iteration 1, all `docs/work/adr-310/experiments/exp-0{1..5}-*.md`:
  - **exp-01 (arbitration ordering)**: natures are state-bound, not changeable — an arc is a state ratchet, not a mutation of nature. Five closed forces (fear/desire/duty/honor/love); default intensity rule ("character is what overrides the strongest impulse").
  - **exp-02 (principles)**: bare lines plus a `define` code ladder; rejected "adjectives imply principles" (tendencies are not commitments); obligations compile to goals; unexcepted collisions default to paralysis; lying is detectable only through D14 belief values.
  - **exp-03 (honor)**: `honor before <scope>` wins ties; rejected folding honor into duty (the Lord Jim case discriminates them); honor sees the room, not the future; found a naming gap between events and topics.
  - **exp-04 (conscience)**: guilt is a ledger of the arbiter's own defeats, banded; two outlets — a D15 selector nudge in-conversation, a D8 goal outside conversation (this dissolves an apparent D16 conflict); sensitivity is a personality adjective; `burdened-by` is a valid initial state; predictability here means ordering, not scheduling.
  - **exp-05 (lie ledger)**: claims are metadata, not new belief kinds — a mint rule plus a selector pin; promises are ledger entries about the character's own future acts; getting caught lying is what unlocks consequences; unraveling a lie is social, routed through D10.
  - Caveat carried in the method notes: this round's "predictability" second-traces were same-session cold re-reads by the same author, not independent readers — a real independent-reader pass is still open.
