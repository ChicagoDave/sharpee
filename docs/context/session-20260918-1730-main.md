## Key Decisions

### 1. The stutter was a replay storm, not paint cost
Per-record delivery gave each record its own dispatcher turn, so the surface kept tripping
its own ordinal-mismatch guard and forking a fresh boot, replaying the whole tree each
time. 720 cards against a settled 169 is the tree replayed about four times over. Batching
removes the storm, which is why it is nine times faster rather than marginally smoother.

### 2. The splitter fix was reverted mid-session, and the revert is the recorded decision
A negative-margin "reach" past the visual track changes where the Grid arranges the
element, producing exactly the two symptoms David reported (jump on drag, one-sided
hit-testing). The fix is a wider real track with a stated `ResizeDirection`, not a wider
hit-test region layered on a narrow one.

### 3. The pane visits one line, not the tree (ADR-353 D1)
An eager whole-tree replay costs the sum of every line's prefix, not the story's size,
and the run column already answers "is this tree green" at 15x the speed through the
CLI. Deleting the eager walk rather than optimizing it is the right shape because the
walk's only unique contribution — live, bindable turn material — only matters for a line
someone is actually looking at.

### 4. A completed turn record does not mean a landed step
The engine returns a normal, completed turn record even when the phase is `stopped` and
the command was refused. A driver that asks only "did a turn land" cannot tell a refusal
from progress. The fix reads the platform's own `story-ending` STATE channel (ADR-347)
instead of inferring the ending from prose or from another client's `data-story-ended`
attribute — both are surrogates for the fact the channel already states directly.

### 5. Phase 17 is superseded, not re-scoped (ADR-353 D8)
Once D1 removes the eager whole-tree replay, there is nothing left to parallelize, no
per-branch seed isolation to preserve under concurrency, and no cross-branch failure
coordination to design — Phase 17's premise is gone, not merely smaller. Phase 16 is
explicitly *not* superseded: a branch entered by click can still reach an ending
mid-prefix, which is a separate concern from the eager walk.

### 6. The label-collision finding is deferred, not patched around
Line identity on the wire is a derived, non-unique label (measured: 31 of 61 secret-letter
lines share a label with another). The correct fix is a stable line id emitted by
`packages/branch-tester` — a platform change requiring David's discussion per CLAUDE.md,
not a client-side workaround folded into ADR-353.
