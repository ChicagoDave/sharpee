## Key Decisions
- Finding emitted from `compileClaims`, not `analyzer.ts`; only things reported. Phase 1 approved by David ("Approved").
- Two passes: firing pass then backward closure. Every claim also reads `ending`. A standard action reads its object's holder chain, trait-setting entities (key, instrument), and conditions of `on` clauses that can refuse it. Walk-chosen holders (`put`/`give`) do not count as reach dependencies; rule-made holders do. `room:*` ("some room") is distinct from `*` so a dropped thing never widens the cone.
