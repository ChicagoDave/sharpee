## Key Decisions

All are David's rulings in the open-questions interview of 2026-11-15; nothing below is built.

### 1. Perception is a system with one home ([ADR-400](../architecture/adrs/adr-400-perception-runs-for-every-actor.md) D1–D5)
`PerceptionService` answers "what does this actor perceive of this event?" as a `Percept`; the funnel asks once per actor; each actor has a perception queue drained at the start of the actor phase and saved in that phase's state; the queue has two readers, `NpcContext.perceived` and the character observer.

### 2. Sight of an act is sight of its actor, for PC and NPC alike (ADR-400 D2 rule 6)
`VisibilityBehavior.canSee(perceiver, actor)` decides, carrying darkness and night vision to NPCs (David: it "has to carry to NPC's too"); a hidden actor's act goes unseen (option A).

### 3. Who hears is who the author marked (ADR-400 D6)
`listener` is unchanged; a heard event joins the listener's perception queue.

### 4. `playerVisible` asks real sight in this landing (ADR-400 D8)
`NpcContext.playerVisible` becomes `world.canSee(npc, player)`; a Dungeo line it moves stops for David's ruling.

### 5. An act the player cannot see is unperceived (ADR-400 D9)
`ISemanticEvent.perceived?: boolean` rides beside `presence`; the default client hides it, the omniscient presentation shows it.

### 6. A perceived line teaches only what the story's clause says (ADR-400 D12)
David chose the authored event over a phrase-to-topic lookup; two new Chord constructs, `after hearing <phrase-key>` and `make <character> know <topic>` (words approved).

### 7. Beyond its room a sound is heard only where authored ([ADR-401](../architecture/adrs/adr-401-hearing-is-authored.md) D1–D2)
`, audible` on an exit line or `acute-hearing` on an actor; ADR-172's every-exit default is superseded (ADR-172 is stamped at the landing, not yet); a sound with no hearer in reach is not propagated.

### 8. An action's descriptor declares a sound tier, off by default (ADR-401 D3)
`use action-sounds` turns it on; the shipped tier table was accepted as drafted: 20 whisper, 9 subdued, 3 normal, the rest silent.

### 9. One side of an act per perceiver, `can hear`, and speech as a located event (ADR-401 D4–D6)
A perceiver gets the act if it can see the actor and its sound if it cannot; Chord gains `<subject> can hear <thing>`, and `ScopeResolver.canHear` moves onto the one hearing rule in that landing; every scene line is a located event with a `speech` sound side.
