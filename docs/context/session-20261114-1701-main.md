## Key Decisions

### 1. ADR-398: Pulling and the press are generic verbs
See `docs/architecture/adrs/adr-398-pulling-and-the-press-are-generic-verbs.md`.
- D1: `PullableTrait` is a marker, and a pull succeeds every time.
- D2: a thing can be pulled, or pressed, when an interceptor (a Chord clause) claims it.
- D3: a push with a direction still needs `pushable`.
- D4: a press on a clause alone does not toggle a switch. This was Claude's call, accepted with the record.
- D5: the words stay, and there is no compile error.
- D6: `already_pulled` and `nothing_happens` are retired.

### 2. The explicit-word compile error was withdrawn
Fifty-nine working fixtures would have needed a trait word only to refuse a verb. David ruled that the explicit rule does not fit generic verbs. Requiring the word again, or adding a diagnostic for its absence, needs a record of its own (ADR-398 Consequences).
