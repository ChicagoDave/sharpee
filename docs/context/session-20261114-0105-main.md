### 3. ADR-396: a `never` claim is watched and searched, and is never called held
Record: [ADR-396](../architecture/adrs/adr-396-a-never-claim-is-watched-and-searched-not-proven.md).
- **Verdicts.** A search can give a `never` one verdict, VIOLATED. Otherwise it reports NOT VIOLATED, a fourth wire value `'not-violated'`, along with what was searched. There is no PROVEN verdict: proof is parked (D6, issue 722).
- **The watch.** Every `never` is watched after every turn of every test-tree line and claims walk (D2), but not in derived tests. A `never` broken on a played line fails the card, and a walk's finding only reports. `ClaimSettledBy` gains `'watch'` (D7a).
- **Needs and exclusions.** Needs on a `never` are optional and are only a search bound, never inherited from the block (D2a). `excluding things:` is allowed on `never` only (D3). A necessity claim's positive twin must name the excluded thing (D3a). Predicates may join facts with `and` (D3b).
- **The walker.** `character.turn` leaves the walk's identity (D4). The walker gains conversation subjects, exchange answers and `wait` (D5), which together are the fix for 717.
- **Report and migration.** The summary line carries two ratios (D7). The existing `never` claims are rewritten in the same change that turns the watch on (D8).
