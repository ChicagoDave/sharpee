## Key Decisions

### 1. Four framings retired before the one that stuck
David supplied every motivating case and retired: (a) a separate display-name field; (b) ADR-107 `nameId` pointing at a Chord phrase (`extendLanguage` drops `phrase.condition`, `loader.ts:994-997`); (c) a core template plus ADR-212 slots (would require opening ADR-255's author-override surface); (d) platform-named components (`enclosure`, `state`) — his own retirement: "we can't assume 'enclosure' or 'state' are relative to what the Author has in mind." The cases that shaped the final decision: the maze, "The Kitchen (at night)"/"(daytime)", the Dungeo barrel, the coal-mine basket, and the well-room bucket (the one D3a-D16a are built around).

### 2. Channels and text blocks are different things
Corrected a conflation I made late in the design: collapsing to one `resolve` (D3a) does not collapse the channel count. `room-name` is the transport that routes the `room.name` block to scrollback; the status channel is a separate surface. Both call the same `resolve`; two channels remain because a scrollback entry and a status value are different things, not because of any "cadence conflict" (which was an artifact of the bad framing, since retracted from the ADR).

### 3. My own error on ADR-350 — corrected in the document
I stated `blocksWalkingMovement` was unenforced. Wrong: `going.ts:102,268` calls `canActorWalkInVehicle`. My grep had excluded the vehicle trait directory. ADR-350 carries a note flagging the correction.
