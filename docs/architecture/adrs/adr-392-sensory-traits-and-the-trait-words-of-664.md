## Context — measured, not assumed

Every line below was read at `0ed88def2` (session f7e980) unless marked.

**Wearing.** `wearable-shared.ts:53-66` (old) blocked a second item on the same body part only when **both** had `layer === undefined`; `WearableTrait`'s constructor set `layer = data.layer ?? 1` **and** `bodyPart = data.bodyPart ?? 'torso'` (`wearableTrait.ts:83, :91`, old). So the check could never fire for a constructed trait, and the naive fix (same part and same layer conflict) would have made every two plain Chord wearables collide, since every one was `'torso'` at layer 1 — UPPS's satchel and space suit, Fernhill's overcoat and anything else. `WearableTrait.canRemove` was declared (`:38`, default `true`) and read by nobody; `wearable-shared.ts:201` read an undeclared `cursed`. A second placement vocabulary — `slot` (default `'clothing'`), `blocksSlots`, `wearableOver` — is read by no action; `WearableBehavior` carries only TODOs against it (`wearableBehavior.ts:45-46, :91`). Chord's `wearable` lowers to `new WearableTrait({})` (`trait-routes.ts:171`) and has no word for a body part.

**Unlocking and switching off.** `unlocking.ts:230-235` read `autoOpenOnUnlock` through a cast and set a message parameter; `switching_off.ts:199-205` read `autoCloseOnOff` and chose the message `'door_closes'` — *"{The target} switches off and closes."* (`lang-en-us/src/actions/switching-off.ts:34`). **Neither opens or closes anything**: no `OpenableBehavior` call and no `isOpen` write in either file. Neither field exists on `IOpenableData`/`OpenableTrait` (`openableTrait.ts:9-72`). `UnlockedEventData.willAutoOpen` is declared (`unlocking-events.ts:44`).

**Smelling and listening.** `scope-resolver.ts:140-145` lets a thing inside a closed container be heard only if `identity.customProperties.loud === true`; `:194-197` smelled only if `customProperties.verySmelly`; `:385-391` gives a thing scent if `smelly`/`verySmelly`, else if edible or actor. `IdentityTrait` declares no `customProperties`, so every read is guarded false. The platform already has **three** sensory scales: ADR-172's `VolumeTier` (`'whisper' | 'subdued' | 'normal' | 'raised' | 'shouting'`, propagated by `engine/src/sound/propagation.ts`, heard by `ListenerTrait` holders), and the witness events' own `WitnessSoundEvent.intensity: 'faint' | 'moderate' | 'loud'` and `WitnessScentEvent.intensity: 'faint' | 'moderate' | 'strong'` (`stdlib/src/scope/types.ts:263, :276`), read by no scope module outside their type file. `AcousticTrait` is a wall material tier, not a thing's sound (`acousticTrait.ts:4-10`). There is no scent model. `PerceptionService` lists smell and hearing as unbuilt senses.

**World Index.** `packages/world-index/src/reach.ts:6-9` models two obstacles, a locked door and a blocked exit. A closed, unlocked door is not one, and `doorStartsLocked` (`loader-semantics.ts:112`) reads only the lock. Nothing here changes either.

## Decision

**D1 — A wearable is simple or placed, and the difference is one required pair.** *(Shipped, `c35339e3e`.)* `WearableTrait.placement?: { bodyPart: string; layer: number }` replaces the free-standing `bodyPart` and `layer`. Absent is **simple**: worn or not, never conflicting, never blocking. Present is **placed**: it conflicts with a worn item on the same `bodyPart` at the same `layer` (`checkWearingConflicts` → `kind: 'same-layer'`), cannot go on under a worn higher layer on that part (`'under-outer'`), and cannot come off from under one (`checkRemovalBlockers`). Both halves are required together: there is no half-placed wearable. One class and one `TraitType` key, because `implementations.ts:99` restores one class per key and about twenty readers fetch `TraitType.WEARABLE` for `worn`/`wornBy` alone; a `SimpleWearableTrait`/`ComplexWearableTrait` split was considered and set aside for that reason (R1a). `hasRemovalRestrictions` reads `!canRemove`. **Phase 3 retires the dead vocabulary** `slot`, `blocksSlots`, `wearableOver` and their `WearableBehavior` helpers (`getSlot`, `getBlockedSlots`), since D1 is the one placement model. **Messages**: the same-layer refusal gets its own id, `already_wearing_there`, and the under-outer refusal `worn_under`, replacing the `'hands_full'` proxy (`wearing.ts:128`); wording is David's at Phase 3 review.

**D2 — Chord says where a wearable sits with `wearable on the <part>`.** The keyless single-value setting shape of ADR-234 D6 / ADR-373 D1, with the preposition `on` and a **bare word** value (`chord.ebnf` composition gains `"wearable" "on" ARTICLE WORD`; the article is required, as `attached to the <thing>` requires it). The word is the author's: the platform compares body parts by equality and nothing more, so a closed anatomy list would be English baked into the grammar for no platform reason. A multi-word part (`on the left hand`) is `WORD { WORD }` joined by spaces, as `setting` already allows (`chord.ebnf:1523`). **IR carrier**: the existing `IRComposition.config` (`chord/src/ir.ts:1031-1050`), one `IRConfigSetting` with `key: 'on'`, `valueKind: 'word'`, `value: '<part>'` (keyed by the preposition so a later `over` can sit beside it; the keyless `''` key stays the entity-valued `with` form's). **Lowering**: the `wearable` route in `trait-routes.ts:171` reads `config.find(c => c.key === 'on')` and builds `new WearableTrait({ placement: { bodyPart: value, layer: 1 } })`; no setting, no placement. **Parser**: `wearable on` not followed by an article is `parse.wearable-on` with the fix-it `wearable on the <part>`; an article with no word after it is the same diagnostic. **No layering word this round** (R2): every Chord wearable is layer 1; the relative form `wearable on the torso over the <thing>` is recorded in the options document as the shape to take when a story asks.

**D3 — An openable can open itself when unlocked and close itself when switched off, and the platform does the opening and closing.** `IOpenableData`/`OpenableTrait` gain `autoOpenOnUnlock?: boolean` and `autoCloseOnOff?: boolean` (default `false`). In **execute**, unlocking a thing that has `autoOpenOnUnlock` and is closed calls `OpenableBehavior.open` after the unlock; switching off a thing that has `autoCloseOnOff` and is open calls `OpenableBehavior.close` after the switch. Report emits the action's own event **and** the opened or closed event (`if.event.opened` / `if.event.closed`, same actor) so a Chord `after the player opening` or `closing` clause on the thing fires — it did open. Messages: unlocking gains `unlocked_and_opened` (and `unlocked_with_and_opened`); switching off keeps `door_closes`, now true. **Whose act it is**: the open and the close are the platform's own consequence of the player's unlock or switch, as a battery coming out darkens a torch (ADR-374 D10) — they run `OpenableBehavior` directly and consult **no** `if.action.opening` / `if.action.closing` interceptor or clause. A story that must stop the door opening writes `on the player unlocking → refuse`, the clause for the action the player actually performed. The `opened`/`closed` event still fires so `after the player opening` reacts to the fact. `UnlockedEventData.willAutoOpen` stays and is set only when the open happened. A message that names a mutation that did not happen is the "dropping bug" class (`docs/core-concepts/README.md`, World State Verification); D3 exists because both branches were exactly that.

**D4 — Chord says it with two lines: `opens when unlocked` and `closes when switched off`.** Entity-block lines beside the adjectives, the shape of `holds <n>`, `servings <n>`, and `<dir> is blocked while <cond>`: one parse rule, one IR flag, one loader route each. **IR carrier**: two entity-line fields on `IREntity`, `opensWhenUnlocked?: true` and `closesWhenSwitchedOff?: true`, beside `holds` and `servings` (`chord/src/ir.ts:499, :506`); absent means false. **Lowering**: the entity builder (`story-loader/src/build/entity.ts`) sets `OpenableTrait.autoOpenOnUnlock` / `.autoCloseOnOff` after trait composition, the way `applyStartsStates` runs after it, so the order of lines does not matter. Analyzer gates: `opens when unlocked` on a thing that is not `lockable` → `analysis.opens-when-not-lockable`; `closes when switched off` on a thing that is not both `switchable` and openable → `analysis.closes-when-not-switchable`. Either line twice on one block → `analysis.duplicate-line`, as `holds` twice is. The adjective form (`self-opening`, `self-closing`) was the runner-up and lowers to the same fields; the line was chosen because the adjective catalog says what a thing *is* and lines say what it *does*. The reaction-clause route (D of the options document) is not sayable today — clause bodies have no statement that opens a thing — and is not this record's business.

**D5 — Scent and sound strength live on two new traits, and each sense has one ruler.**
- `ScentTrait` (`world-model/src/traits/scent/scentTrait.ts`): `export type ScentStrength = 'faint' | 'moderate' | 'strong'; interface IScentData { strength?: ScentStrength; descriptionId?: string }`, constructor default `strength: 'moderate'`. The scale **is** the witness scent scale (`scope/types.ts:276`); `WitnessScentEvent.intensity: ScentStrength` imports the type from world-model rather than restating it.
- `SoundingTrait` (`world-model/src/traits/sounding/soundingTrait.ts`): `interface ISoundingData { tier?: VolumeTier; descriptionId?: string }`, constructor default `tier: 'normal'`, `VolumeTier` imported from `@sharpee/if-domain` (already a world-model dependency, `packages/world-model/package.json:31`) — a thing that is loud and a sound that is shouting are measured on ADR-172's one ruler. `WitnessSoundEvent.intensity` becomes a **projection** of `VolumeTier`: `export type WitnessSoundIntensity = 'faint' | 'moderate' | 'loud'; export function witnessSoundIntensityOf(tier: VolumeTier): WitnessSoundIntensity` in `stdlib/src/scope/types.ts` (`whisper`/`subdued` → `faint`, `normal` → `moderate`, `raised`/`shouting` → `loud`), so the three-tier scale is derived and cannot drift.
- Scope reads them and nothing else: `canSmell`'s closed-container rule passes at `strength === 'strong'`; `hasScent` is true when a `ScentTrait` is present, else the implicit faint scent of an edible or an actor (today's fallback, kept); `canHear`'s closed-container rule passes at `tier` ≥ `'raised'`. The three `customProperties` reads are deleted.
- `descriptionId` is unused now. It is where the 5.0 per-sense prose line lands (`smells of …`, `sounds like …`), so that round adds a field's writer, not a trait.
- Both are plain data traits (ADR-346 D1), both barrels and `dist`/`dist-esm` rebuilt (world-model root barrel discipline), registered in `implementations.ts` so they restore.

**D6 — Chord gets two bare adjectives now, `smelly` and `loud`; the prose lines stay 5.0.** `smelly` → `new ScentTrait({ strength: 'strong' })`; `loud` → `new SoundingTrait({ tier: 'raised' })`. Both join `TRAIT_ADJECTIVES` (`catalog.ts:35`), one route each in `trait-routes.ts`, no gate (any thing may smell or sound). Whatever generated manifest or catalog lists trait adjectives (`packages/chord/src/manifests/`, the IDE's docs catalog) is regenerated by its owning `./repokit` step, never hand-edited; `chord.ebnf` and its pin are re-recorded; one dated row goes into `docs/architecture/chord-grammar-changes.md` covering D2, D4 and D6 together. The old `smelly`/`verySmelly` two-level distinction collapses to one word meaning "escapes a closed container"; the implicit faint scent needs no word. `quiet`, `faint` and the prose lines are the 5.0 senses row's, on these traits.

**D7 — No new Chord word for the rows the clause already covers, and none for capacity.** An unremovable wearable is `canRemove: false` on the trait (TypeScript stories, the golden test) and `on the player taking off → refuse <phrase>` in Chord (R7). An NPC refusing a gift is `on the player giving → refuse` (R8); `customProperties.preferences` stays a TypeScript knob. No carrying-capacity word, per the 2026-10-12 holdall ruling. Talking's pre-ADR-320 `customProperties.conversation` surface is **not** decided here (R9: its own issue).

**D8 — Save, restore and the World Index are untouched by design.** The new fields are trait data the entity serialization already carries (ADR-374's precedent for `BackdropTrait.roomIds`); no save version moves (save-format versioning is dormant). Neither D3 field changes `doorStartsLocked` or the edge set, so Reach is unchanged; Phase 4 still re-runs ADR-321 AC-6 because it adds loader semantics (D2, D4, D6 are new rows in the loader's tables).

## Consequences

- **Two shapes of wearable are now visible to the type checker**, and the sentinel bug class (`layer === undefined` on a defaulted field) cannot recur: a placed wearable has both halves or it is not placed.
- **Three dead or half-built platform knobs become real features** with Chord words: body-part conflicts, auto-open, auto-close. Each is an IF-standard convenience a story can now say in one line.
- **Loudness has one ruler.** A later feature that asks "how loud is this thing" reads `VolumeTier`; the witness three-tier is a view of it. Scent has one ruler too, the one the witness events already had.
- **The 5.0 senses round starts from these traits**, writing `descriptionId` and adding the lower tiers' words, rather than from nothing.
- **`if.event.switched_on` lost `lightIntensity`** (Phase 2, no source for it) and `if.event.unlocked` / `if.event.switched_off` gain a sibling `opened`/`closed` event when D3 fires. Event handlers keyed on those types see one more event per such turn.
- **Chord grammar grows by one setting form, two lines and two adjectives**, logged as one dated row in `chord-grammar-changes.md` (David's "proceed" of 2026-11-08 is the approval); `chord.ebnf` and its pin are re-recorded; the language version does not move (ADR-257, additive).
- **Dungeo** writes no Chord and declares none of these fields; its transcripts are the regression check that the wearable, unlocking and switching paths it does exercise moved nothing (its wearables are simple).
- **Not decided here**: layering in Chord (D2's deferral), per-sense prose (5.0), talking's legacy conversation property (R9), the liquid-in-a-container seam (#650).

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1 — Two hats, one head.** In the test story, `wear cap` while the hat is worn refuses with `already_wearing_there` naming the hat; `wear scarf` (another part) succeeds. *(stdlib `wearing-golden`; tree `stories/trait-field-test/trait-field-test.tests/`.)*
- **AC-2 — Simple wearables never conflict.** Two wearables with no placement are both worn; a placed one ignores a simple one. *(`wearing-golden.test.ts` — "two simple wearables (no placement) never conflict", "a placed wearable ignores a simple one on no part"; SELF-VERIFYING, shipped.)*
- **AC-3 — Unlock opens.** `unlock door with key` on an `opens when unlocked` door leaves `OpenableTrait.isOpen === true` and emits `if.event.unlocked` and `if.event.opened`; the same door without the line stays closed. *(stdlib `unlocking-golden` asserts `isOpen`; tree.)*
- **AC-4 — Switch off closes.** `turn off case` on a `closes when switched off` open container leaves `isOpen === false` and emits `if.event.closed`; without the line it stays open and `door_closes` is never chosen. *(stdlib `switching_off-golden` asserts `isOpen`; tree.)*
- **AC-5 — A strong scent escapes a closed box; a faint one does not.** `smell` from the room with the `smelly` cheese in a closed box lists the cheese; an edible with no `ScentTrait` in the same box is not smelled. *(stdlib `scope-integration`; tree.)*
- **AC-6 — A loud thing is heard from a closed container and the next room; a normal one is not heard from the container.** *(stdlib `scope-integration`; tree.)*
- **AC-7 — No undeclared read.** `grep -rn -E "as unknown as Record<string, unknown>" packages/stdlib/src/actions/standard/{wearable-shared,unlocking/unlocking,switching_off/switching_off,switching-shared,touching/touching,giving/giving,talking/talking}.ts packages/stdlib/src/scope/scope-resolver.ts` returns nothing, and `grep -rn "664" packages/stdlib/tests` returns nothing. *(SELF-VERIFYING — the grep is the claim.)*
- **AC-8 — The World Index still reads the loader the same way.** ADR-321 AC-6's loader-semantics pins pass after Phase 4; `wearable on`, the two lines and the two adjectives appear in no Reach rule. *(world-index tests.)*
- **AC-9 — No trait constructor throws.** ADR-346 AC-1's grep still returns exactly its one `characterModelTrait.ts` hit after `ScentTrait` and `SoundingTrait` land. *(SELF-VERIFYING.)*
- **AC-10 — The witness sound scale is derived.** A test maps every `VolumeTier` through `witnessSoundIntensityOf` and asserts the three-tier result; `WitnessSoundEvent.intensity`'s type is `WitnessSoundIntensity`, not a restated union. *(stdlib `scope` tests.)*
- **AC-11 — The words refuse where they do not belong.** Chord compiler tests: `opens when unlocked` on a block that is not `lockable` → `analysis.opens-when-not-lockable`; `closes when switched off` on a block that is not both `switchable` and openable → `analysis.closes-when-not-switchable`; `wearable on head` (no article) and `wearable on the` (no part) → `parse.wearable-on`; each well-formed line on the right block compiles clean and the IR carries the field or setting named in D2/D4. *(chord `tests/`; story-loader tests assert the lowered trait fields.)*
- **AC-12 — Auto-open and auto-close are idempotent and emit only what happened.** Unlocking an `autoOpenOnUnlock` thing that is already open emits `if.event.unlocked` and no `if.event.opened`; switching off an `autoCloseOnOff` thing that is already closed emits `if.event.switched_off` and no `if.event.closed`, and `door_closes` is not chosen (`switching_off-golden.test.ts` "already closed" fixture). *(stdlib goldens assert `isOpen` and the event list.)*
