# ADR-342: `@sharpee/engine` has a contract — `index.ts` names what consumers use

**Status**: **ACCEPTED** (David, 2026-09-09, session 42e176 — "accept" after `adr-review` returned 16/16 with three folds: a citation line range, a durable citation for the 112-name count, and the repointed test named in Scope. ## Decision

- **D1 — The contract is the consumer union plus the authoring surface, one name at a time.** `index.ts` contains no `export *`. It names exactly these 31 exports:
  - *The facade*: `GameEngine`, `EngineConfig`, `DEFAULT_TEXT_CAPABILITIES`.
  - *What a story author writes against*: `Story`, `StoryConfig`, `StoryWithEvents`, `validateStoryConfig`, `CustomVocabulary`, `NarrativeConfig`, `ParsedCommandTransformer`, `InputModeHandler`, `SharedDataKeys`.
  - *What a turn hands back*: `GameContext`, `TurnResult`.
  - *The turn as a named list (ADR-334)*: `TURN_STAGES`, `META_STAGES`, `SHARED_STAGES`, and their element type `TurnStage`.
  - *Services a host or a test drives directly*: `CommandExecutor`, `createCommandExecutor`, `EngineRandomService`, `SaveRestoreService`, `ISaveRestoreStateProvider`, `SAVE_FORMAT_VERSION`, `VocabularyManager`.
  - *Plugins*: `ActorTurnPlugin`, `ACTOR_TURN_PLUGIN_ID`, and `PluginRegistry` re-exported from `@sharpee/plugins` for as long as story-loader's tests take it from here.
  - *Seams another package calls by name*: `phrasebookTemplateKey`, `PhrasebookResolution` (story-loader), `lintUnusedSnippetEntries` (devkit).

  **Amendment A1 (2026-09-09, session 42e176 — ADR-343 ACCEPTED)**: `StoryEngine` joins the list, making it **32 names**. `Story.onEngineReady` takes the role instead of the `GameEngine` class, and fourteen implementers name the parameter type, so D3's trigger is met — a type a consumer must *name* is added, and this line is the record of the decision. Declared in `install/story.ts` beside `Story`; the facade `implements` it.

  Thirty of the thirty-one are the inventory (26) plus the four stage names the plan carries from ADR-334. **`EngineConfig` is the one addition made by judgment**: it is the host's construction contract, `StoryConfig`'s counterpart on the other side of `new GameEngine(...)`, and a host that types its options at all has to name it. No other name is added on the strength of "someone might want it"; D3 says what happens when someone does.

- **D2 — Eighty-one names stop being exported**, by group, with the reason:
  - *Capability dispatch helpers* (9: `CapabilityClaim`, `CapabilityDispatchCheck`, `CapabilityDispatchData`, `checkCapabilityDispatch`, `checkCapabilityDispatchMulti`, `executeCapabilityValidate/Execute/Report/Blocked`) — called by the command executor only; stdlib's dispatch factory cannot depend on the engine.
  - *Parser port* (5: `IEngineAwareParser`, `isEngineAwareParser`, `hasWorldContext`, `hasPronounContext`, `hasPlatformEventEmitter`) — the engine's probes of what it was given; `parser-en-us` satisfies the shape without importing it. Phase 6 decides where the adapter lives; nothing here pre-empts that.
  - *Story installation* (12: `InstallStep`, `InstallContext`, `StoryInstallDraft`, `StoryInstallResult`, `StoryMetadata`, `runInstallSteps`, `STORY_INSTALL_STEPS`, `configureLanguageProviderNarrative`, `validateRoomSnippets`, `SnippetValidationError`, `validateCombatantHealth`, `CombatantHealthValidationError`) — `installStory`'s pipeline; the order test that pins it lives inside the package. devkit's `error?.name === 'SnippetValidationError'` check is a string contract on the error's name, which does not change.
  - *Narrative internals* (5: `Perspective`, `Tense`, `NarrativeSettings`, `DEFAULT_NARRATIVE_SETTINGS`, `buildNarrativeSettings`) — what `NarrativeConfig` resolves to; `getNarrativeSettings()` returns it unnamed under D3.
  - *Turn internals* (10: `runTurnStages`, `requiresOrderViolations`, `ROUTE_STAGE`, `OrderViolation`, `splitChainedInput`, `wasRefused`, `TurnStageContext`, `TurnEngine`, `StageOutcome`, `TurnRoute`) — the runner and the seam the facade hands its stages; a story cannot add a stage, so nothing outside needs the context a stage runs in.
  - *Turn event processing* (6: `EventProcessingContext`, `TurnEnrichment`, `TurnEventSource`, `enrichTurnEvents`, `processEvent`, `transactionIdFor`) and *platform dispatch* (4: `PlatformEventDelivery`, `PlatformOperationHost`, `dispatchPlatformOperations`, `platformOperationFailure`) — stage bodies.
  - *Sound propagation* (4: `SoundDispatcher`, `AUDIBILITY_HEARD_EVENT_TYPE`, `propagate`, `clarityToTier`) — ADR-172's machinery behind `emitPlatformEvent` and the stages; no consumer.
  - *Session internals* (5: `createSaveRestoreService`, `UndoConfig`, `createVocabularyManager`, `ACTION_STREAM_POINT_NAME`, `TURN_STREAM_POINT_NAME`) — factories the facade calls and stream-point names the random service registers.
  - *`types.ts` residue* (6: `TimingData`, `MetaCommandResult`, `CommandResult`, `INPUT_MODE_STATE_KEY`, and the `IPerceptionService`/`Sense` pass-through of `@sharpee/stdlib`'s types, `types.ts:12`) — field types of `TurnResult` and a re-export whose home is `@sharpee/if-services`.
  - *Command executor internals* (3: `ActorCommand`, `BeforeActionHookData`, `BeforeActionHookListener`) — parameter types of `onBeforeAction` and `executeAsActor`, unnamed under D3.
  - *Facade, introspection, plugins, shared data* (12: `GameEngineEvents`; `EngineIntrospection`, `ActionSummary`, `TraitSummary`, `BehaviorBindingSummary`, `MessageSummary`; `SceneEvaluationPlugin`, `LEGACY_NPC_PLUGIN_ID`, `TurnPlugin`, `TurnPluginContext`; `SharedDataKeyType`, `EngineSharedData`) — the event map and the introspection read-model are return shapes (D3); the scene plugin is registered by the engine itself; the legacy id is a read-side save alias; `TurnPlugin` and `TurnPluginContext` belong to `@sharpee/plugins`, where a story that writes one already imports them.

- **D3 — Reachable is not named.** A type that appears only inside a kept export's signature — `IProsePipeline`, `EngineIntrospection`, `NarrativeSettings`, `TimingData`, `BeforeActionHookListener`, and the rest — stays internal. Consumers compile, because declaration emit refers to the declaring module by path; a consumer that must *name* one of them is the trigger for adding it, and that addition is a decision recorded as an amendment line on D1, not a barrel edit. This is the plan's stated consequence: a new module is internal until a consumer needs it, and adding it to `index.ts` is the decision.

- **D4 — The root barrel names declaring modules, never a sub-barrel.** `turn/index.ts`, `install/index.ts`, `install/narrative/index.ts`, and `sound/index.ts` stay as the package's internal conveniences, and `index.ts` imports each kept name from the module that declares it. A name added to a sub-barrel therefore cannot leak. No new sub-barrel is required; Phase 7's open question is answered by keeping the four that exist internal.

- **D5 — The name set is pinned by a test.** `packages/engine/tests/unit/public-surface.test.ts` resolves the export set of `src/index.ts` through the TypeScript checker (the same method Phase 7 used) and asserts it equals D1's list; an export that appears or disappears fails by name. The same shape as ADR-334 D2's stage-order test and ADR-340 D3's ownership test: the invariant is a test, not a comment.

- **D6 — No behavior changes.** The gate is byte-identical (Dungeo chain, the three Chord trees, the engine suite); `GameEngine`'s members are untouched (ADR-334 AC-5); every consumer in the inventory compiles without an edit. The two dead examples in the Context are noted, not touched.

## Consequences

- The engine package has an inside. The next extraction out of the facade lands in `command/`, `install/`, `session/`, `turn/`, or `ports/` without becoming public API, which is what Phases 7 and 8 were for ("so that the next extraction out of the facade has somewhere to go that is not public API", plan header).
- Adding an export is a recorded decision: an amendment line on D1 and a fixture change in the D5 test, in the same commit. Removing one is likewise a decision, checked by the same test and by the consumer builds.
- The generated reference the IDE ships to authors describes the contract rather than the internals; its diff at this phase is the record of what left.
- Three story-loader tests keep taking `PluginRegistry` from the engine; repointing them to `@sharpee/plugins` and retiring that re-export is a small follow-up, not part of this phase.
- `index.ts` carries a rule-9 header naming the contract and its owner (`documentationStandard: always`, `.devarch/descriptor.json`).

## Acceptance

- **AC-1 (no wildcard)**: `grep -c 'export \*' packages/engine/src/index.ts` prints `0`.
- **AC-2 (the set)**: the checker-based export map of `src/index.ts` prints exactly the 31 names in D1, and the D5 test passes; a scratch `export { wasRefused }` added to `index.ts` fails it by name.
- **AC-3 (every consumer compiles)**: `./repokit build dungeo` succeeds and `./sharpee test branch-stories/<story>` builds each of the three Chord trees; no file in the inventory is edited.
- **AC-4 (the record)**: `tsc --build --force` on `packages/engine` and its dependents, then `node scripts/generate-genai-api.js`; the `engine.md` diff — declaration headings before and after — is reviewed by David.
- **AC-5 (byte-identical)**: the Dungeo walkthrough chain and the three Chord trees are byte-identical to the Phase 7 baseline after stripping timings; the engine suite reports no failures and one more test than Phase 7's 750 passing / 7 skipped.
