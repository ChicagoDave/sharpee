## Context — measured, not assumed

**Transparency exists on the platform and nothing in Chord reaches it.** `ContainerTrait.isTransparent` (`packages/world-model/src/traits/container/containerTrait.ts:29`, default `false`) is read by `VisibilityBehavior.addVisibleContents` (`world/VisibilityBehavior.ts:376` and `:472`): a closed container whose trait is transparent keeps its contents visible, so they are in scope and `examine` lists them. The loader builds every container bare (`loader.ts:1506`, `new ContainerTrait()`), and `transparent` is not an adjective (`catalog.ts` `TRAIT_ADJECTIVES`; probed 2026-10-13: `a container, transparent` is `analysis.trait-not-declared`). So every Chord container is opaque, as the checklist says. One platform reader does not consult the flag: `searching` (`stdlib/src/actions/standard/searching/searching.ts:104`) refuses any closed openable container with `container_closed`, transparent or not, and `look in <x>` is a searching shape (`parser-en-us/grammar/standard-en-us.story:30`). Dungeo sets the flag nowhere (its one mention is the GDT debug printer), so a change to that reader cannot change Dungeo's text.

**Capacity is sayable today, under two spellings the checklist missed.** The analyzer accepts any `with <key> <value>` setting on a kind noun and the loader reads three: `a container with max items <n>` and `with max weight <n>` (`loader.ts:2157`, `applyContainerConfig`, pinned by `story-loader/tests/loader.test.ts:216`) and `a supporter with capacity <n>` (`loader.ts:2553`). The corpus uses them: fernhill's travelling trunk (`a container with max items 3`, `fernhill.story:355`) and mantel (`a supporter with capacity 2, scenery`, `:389`), and the zoo's park bench (`a supporter with capacity 3, scenery`, `zoo.story:400`). The checklist row 126 ("none") is wrong and is corrected by this record. The same probe shows the gap under it: `a container with capacity 2` compiles clean and does nothing, because no analyzer gate knows which settings a kind noun takes. Weight has no Chord word at all (`weight` appears nowhere in the catalog or grammar), so `max weight` is a setting no story can make matter. The platform check is `ContainerBehavior.canAccept` → `checkCapacity` (`containerBehavior.ts:88-118`), consulted by `putting` (`putting.ts:223`), which `inserting` delegates to; a full container refuses with `no_room` ("There's no room in {the container}.", `lang-en-us/src/actions/putting.ts:31`).

**`in` and `out` are platform directions Chord cannot write.** `Direction.IN`/`OUT` exist with each other as opposites (`world-model/src/constants/directions.ts:19-20, 39-40`), the English parser maps `in`, `inside`, `out`, `outside` to them (`parser-en-us/src/direction-mappings.ts:24-27`), and the loader's `toDirection` resolves any Chord direction word through the enum (`loader.ts:2541`). Chord's `DIRECTIONS` set (`parser.ts:228`) and the EBNF's `DIRECTION` (`chord.ebnf:1130`) stop at the compass and `up`/`down`, so `in to the Hall` on a room is read as a composition line and fails as an undeclared trait (GH #568's repro, re-probed 2026-10-13). The one reader that cannot take them unchanged is the map index: `STEP` (`world-index/src/map.ts:38`) has a cell offset per compass direction and `in`/`out` have no bearing, and an edge with no step is skipped (`:211`), so a room reachable only by `in`/`out` would be left in `unplaced`.

**Why `in to` is unambiguous.** A placement line is `in <article> <name>` (`in the Hall`, `chord.ebnf` `placement`), and the parser's placement branch requires an article in the second position (`parser.ts:2076`). `in to the Hall` has `to` there, so it falls through to the direction branch, which tests `DIRECTIONS.has(word) && isWord('to', 1)` (`:2150`). The same branch order already separates `on the table` (placement) from `on the player taking` (a head), so no new rule is needed.

## Decision

**D1 — `transparent` is a container adjective.** `a container, transparent` (or `transparent` on its own composition line) lowers to `ContainerTrait.isTransparent = true` on the entity the loader builds. It is legal only on a block whose kind is `container` or `vehicle` (ADR-366; a vehicle is a container) — `analysis.transparent-not-container` elsewhere, naming the block's kind — and only unconditionally: `transparent while …` is `analysis.transparent-conditional`, because the flag is a property of the thing, not a state the story toggles (as `proper` is, ADR-242 D1). It joins `TRAIT_ADJECTIVES` in the catalog as a marker, as `concealed` and `unlisted` did: no trait of its own, one field on the kind's trait.

**D1a — `searching` and `examining` read transparency.** `search <x>` and `look in <x>` on a closed container whose trait is transparent list its contents instead of refusing; the refusal `container_closed` stands for a closed opaque one (`searching.ts`'s validate adds `&& !ContainerBehavior.isTransparent(target)` to its closed check). `examine <x>` on the same container prints its contents line: the Context's claim that visibility alone made examine list them was wrong by one step — `VisibilityBehavior` put the contents in scope, but examining's message builder printed the contents line only when `isOpen` (`examining-data.ts`, `buildExaminingMessageParams`), found by the UPPS probe on 2026-10-13; it now prints it when the container is open or transparent. These are the platform changes in the record, both readers of one flag, so after D1a every way a player looks inside agrees. Dungeo's text cannot move (no Dungeo container is transparent); the chain is run to confirm (AC-9).

**D2b — Inserting into a full container says so.** Found by the same probe: `insert`/`put … in` past capacity refused correctly but rendered blank, because the refusal comes from the delegated putting validate as `no_room` and is reported under inserting's id, which the language layer had no entry for. `inserting` now declares `no_room` among its messages and lang-en-us carries "There's no room in {the container}."; the alias catalog gains `inserting-no-room`. Not a Chord change; recorded here because D2's "nothing new is narrated" assumed the message existed.

**D2 — `holds <n>` is a line, and the one spelling of capacity.** A `container`, `supporter` or `vehicle` block takes one `holds <n>` line among its create-lines, `n` a whole number of one or more, lowered to `capacity.maxItems` on `ContainerTrait` (container, vehicle) or `SupporterTrait` (supporter) — the field `canAccept` already checks, so `put`/`insert` into a full one refuses with `no_room` and nothing new is narrated.

```ebnf
holds-line   = "holds" NUMBER NL ;                         (* ADR-369 D2: container,
                                                              supporter and vehicle
                                                              blocks only; at most one;
                                                              NUMBER >= 1 *)
```

It is a line, not a `with` setting, because `a container with holds 3` is not English and every setting today names a value of an adjective (`lockable with the iron key`), while `holds 3` is a fact about the thing in the same register as `states:` and `counter`. Gates: `holds` on any other kind is `analysis.holds-owner` (naming the three kinds); a second `holds` line is `analysis.holds-duplicate`; `holds 0`, a negative, a fraction or a word is `parse.holds-number`. On a `person` it is refused like any other kind: a playable character's ten-item capacity (`loader.ts:1522`) is the platform's and is not this record's to expose.

**D2a — The three settings retire with fix-its, and the corpus migrates mechanically.** `with max items <n>` and `with max weight <n>` on `a container`, and `with capacity <n>` on `a supporter`, become `parse.removed-capacity-setting`, whose message shows the `holds <n>` line (for `max weight`: that weight is not a Chord concept, and `holds <n>` counts items). The corpus lines are rewritten in the same change — fernhill's trunk and mantel with David's go (they are his story; the edit is syntax, not prose), the zoo's bench by Claude — and the two loader readers (`applyContainerConfig`, `supporterCapacity`) are deleted with their test rewritten to `holds`. *Implementation note (2026-10-13)*: the Context's count of three was the stories only; a repo-wide search found thirteen copies — the Cloak of Darkness hook (`stories/cloak-of-darkness/cloak.story`), four chord test fixtures (`cloak`, `comments-golden`, `zoo-phase-c`, the ADR-362 fold test's chest), the IDE's frozen fernhill fixture (`tools/ide/test-fixtures/fernhill-frozen`), the published fernhill learn page (`website/src/app/learn/fernhill/things`), and three test expectations — all migrated in the same change. No other kind setting is read by the loader (`configValue` callers measured 2026-10-13: `text` and `position` are adjective settings, the three above are the only kind settings), so after D2a **a kind noun takes no `with` settings**, and `a <kind> with …` is `parse.kind-setting` pointing at the adjective the author meant. That closes the silent-no-op gap the Context measured without a separate issue.

**D3 — `in` and `out` are exit directions.** `in to <Room>` and `out to <Room>` are exit lines in every form an exit line has: plain (the reverse is inferred — `in to the Closet` on the Hall gives the Closet `out to the Hall`, through `DirectionOpposites`), `, one-way`, `through <door>`, on an `exits:` line (ADR-362 D2) and in a region's exit table (ADR-360 D3), and `in is blocked …` / `out is deadly …` as blocked and deadly lines. The parser's `DIRECTIONS` set and the EBNF's `DIRECTION` gain the two words; `toDirection` already resolves them; `analysis.duplicate-exit` (ADR-360 D3a) covers them as any direction. An exit on a non-room stays `analysis.exit-non-room`. `in <article> <name>` stays placement (Context: the article decides, as it does today for `on`).

```ebnf
DIRECTION    = "north" | "south" | "east" | "west"
             | "northeast" | "northwest" | "southeast" | "southwest"
             | "up" | "down" | "in" | "out" ;                (* ADR-369 D3 *)
```

**D3a — Typing `in` or `out` is going, as it is today.** The English parser already turns `in`, `inside`, `out`, `outside` into the going action's direction (Context), so a room with an `in` exit answers `in`, `go in` and `inside` with no grammar change. `enter <thing>` and `exit` remain the entering and exiting actions on enterable things (ADR-218 §1a); this record does not fold them into exits, because the two mean different things to the platform (an enterable is a place inside a room; an `in` exit is a room).

**D3b — The map gives `in`/`out` no bearing and never loses a room to them.** `in` and `out` have no compass offset, so the map index does not step for them. A room whose every exit is `in`/`out` would otherwise stay in `unplaced`; instead the index places it in the first free cell beside the room its `out` (or, failing that, its `in`) exit leads to, using the collision search `MAX_DISPLACEMENT_RADIUS` already bounds, and records the edge so the map draws it. The IDE's map view draws such an edge as it draws a skew: a line between the two rooms, labelled with the direction.

**D4 — No new IR shape.** `transparent` rides `IREntity.traits` as a marker name, as `unlisted` does; `holds` rides `IREntity` as an optional additive `holds?: number` field; `in`/`out` ride `IRExit.direction` as two more strings. `IR_FORMAT` does not move.

## Consequences

- **Three checklist rows become sayable, and one is corrected.** Row 119 (transparent container) and row 112 (`in`/`out` exits) move from none/partial to sayable; row 126 (capacity) is corrected from "none" to "sayable under two spellings, replaced by `holds <n>`", with the evidence pointer naming the UPPS cards.
- **One spelling of capacity, and no silent kind settings.** `holds <n>` is the only way to bound a container or supporter; the three retired settings and every other `with` on a kind noun are errors that name the fix. Three corpus lines change (two in fernhill, with David's go).
- **One stdlib reader changes** (D1a), so the Dungeo chain is run once; the change cannot reach Dungeo's text because no Dungeo container is transparent.
- **The map index learns a placement rule** (D3b) rather than a bearing; the world index's room data is unchanged.
- **GH #568 closes** with D3; the UNDERGROUND port's room 72 can be written.
- **The language surface changes without a version bump**: `holds-line`, the two directions and the removal are re-recorded in the pin under the unpublished 4.0 surface (ADR-257 as amended), with a grammar-log row per decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1 (D1)**: `a container, transparent` compiles, and the loader's entity has `ContainerTrait.isTransparent === true`; `a thing, transparent` is `analysis.transparent-not-container`; `transparent while …` is `analysis.transparent-conditional`.

- **AC-4 (D2, REAL-PATH)**: on a UPPS line, inserting a second item into the one-slot card slot refuses with `no_room`, and the first insert succeeds; the tree card pins the refusal text.

- **AC-7 (D3a, REAL-PATH)**: on a UPPS line, `out` from the Airlock with the hatch open moves the player to the Landing Pad, `in` from the pad returns, and `out` with the hatch closed speaks the blocked phrase.
