An entity (or trait, or the story itself) can declare named states with a states: line. The owner starts in the first state listed. States are the language's replacement for flags and ad-hoc booleans: they are declared, ordered, and visible to conditions (when the fountain is still), select on dispatch, and change.

create the fountain
  a thing, scenery
  in the Orangery
  states, reversible: flowing, still

  A marble fountain in the middle of the floor.

  on the player touching
    change the fountain to still when the fountain is flowing
    phrase fountain-stopped when the fountain is still
  end on

State order is a promise. Without , reversible, a change back to an earlier state is a load error (analysis.irreversible-state): a marrow declared states: entire, sliced can never be un-sliced, and the compiler holds you to it. Add , reversible only when the story really does move both ways, as the fountain does.

One name is reserved. dark is the platform's word for a room without light, so a states: line on anything but a room may not declare a state called dark (analysis.dark-state-reserved): the lantern is dark would read the room's light, never the lantern's state. A thing that gives light composes light-source (with switchable to turn it on and off) instead of declaring a dark state.
