This chapter covers moving things by hand: picking them up, putting them down, putting them in and on other things, handing them over, and shoving them around. Everything in this chapter works out of the box. The point of each entry is to tell you what the platform already does, which trait (if any) makes an entity eligible, and which message keys to override when you want it said differently.

Each entry lists the action's message keys, the IDs stdlib emits instead of English (the lang layer supplies the words). Override one per entity with an on/after clause carrying your own phrase (chord-language.md), or story-wide with a define phrase under the dotted ID itself (dotted keys register whole since ADR-230 D5, see chord-language.md; a TypeScript story overrides the ID through the language provider). In transcripts and event payloads, the ID is how you recognize which moment fired. The worked example in taking and dropping shows the per-entity guard and the story-wide override side by side. The same two seams recur all through this chapter.

  • taking and dropping
  • putting and inserting
  • removing (taking from)
  • giving and showing
  • throwing
  • pushing, pulling, touching
  • lowering and raising
  • cutting and digging
