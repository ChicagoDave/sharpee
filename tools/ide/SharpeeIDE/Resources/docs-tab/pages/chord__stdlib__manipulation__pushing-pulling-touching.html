push (pushing) covers the verbs push, press, shove, move, each with or without a direction (push X north, shove X east, move X up). Needs the pushable trait; pushing anything else gets fixed_in_place (scenery) or pushing_does_nothing. With no direction the push is a press: if the thing is also switchable, pushing toggles it (button_clicks when it is a button, switch_toggled otherwise), and anything else just takes the push (button_pushed) and lets its on the player pushing clause say what happened. With a direction the push moves the thing through the room's exit into the next room, with the player, when the way admits pushing (ADR-378): admits pushing on the exit line or the room's admits: list for any pushable thing, or the thing's own pushing block of room pairs for that one thing. The player's own step is the going action's, so every refusal going knows refuses the push in going's words — no exit, a hidden way, the room's blocked phrase, a closed or locked door, a vehicle the player is riding — and both on the player pushing on the thing and on the player going on the room fire. A way that exists but does not admit pushing refuses cannot_push_that_way; a carried thing refuses carrying_it. On success pushed_direction prints, then the new room's description with the thing in it. The trait carries no fields: the thing says it can be pushed, the way says whether a push moves it.

pull (pulling) covers the verbs pull, drag, yank, tug. Needs pullable. A successful pull sets the trait's state to pulled (a second pull refuses already_pulled) and bumps its pullCount; a worn item refuses worn. Everything a pull means (the lever opens the sluice) is story logic reacting to pulled or written as an on the player pulling / after the player pulling clause.

touch (touching) covers the verbs touch, feel, rub, pat, stroke, poke, prod. No trait needed, no state changed; the reply is inferred from what the thing is: a lit light source feels_hot, a running device feels_warm (or device_vibrating), wearables feels_soft, doors feels_smooth, containers and supporters feels_hard (a container with drink in it sloshes, liquid_container), drinkables feels_wet, scenery immovable_object. Otherwise the verb itself answers (poked, patted, stroked, touched_gently, touched).
