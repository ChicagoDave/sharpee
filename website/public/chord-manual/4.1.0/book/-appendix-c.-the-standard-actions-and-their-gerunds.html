Appendix C. The standard actions and their gerunds

Every action the platform ships: 80 actions, 77 of them with typed commands. The heading of each entry is the gerund a clause names (on the player taking), and the block under it is that action’s entry in the standard grammar, as shipped. Generated from the standard grammar (Sharpee 5.7.1 · Chord 4.1.0) by scripts/generate-chord-appendices.cjs.
