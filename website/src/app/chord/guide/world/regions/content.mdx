A region groups rooms and owns behavior for all of them. A room joins a
region with an `in the <region>` line in its own block, and a region can
declare a set of identical rooms itself with a `rooms` group; a room is in
at most one region, and regions do not nest. An `on every turn` clause is a
region daemon firing only while the player is in a member room, and
`after the player entering` / `after the player leaving` bind to boundary
crossings in either direction. `leaving` exists only on region blocks.

```chord
create the Grounds
  a region
  landing the Drive

  on every turn while one chance in 6
    phrase night-wind
  end on

  after the player entering
    phrase cold-returns
  end after

  after the player leaving
    phrase out-of-the-wind
  end after

create the Drive
  a room
  in the Grounds

  A gravel drive.

create the Hall
  a room
  in the Grounds

  A cold hall.
```

Identical rooms, such as the rooms of a maze, are declared once with their
count: `rooms Maze 1 to 12` inside the region creates `the Maze 1` to
`the Maze 12`, each a member, each taking the group's heading and
description as its own. A room that needs anything of its own, a clause
or different text, is written as its own block with an `in` line instead.

```chord
create the Maze
  a region

  rooms Maze 1 to 12
    room name:
      Maze

    You are in a maze of twisty little passages, all alike.
```

A region becomes a *place* once it says where things land. `landing the
Drive` names the room that `move … to the Grounds` puts an entity in,
and it is what `the Grounds's location` means. A list must say how to
choose — `landing, randomly: the Drive, the Hall` — with `cycling` and
`stopping` as the other two strategy words, drawn on the region's own
seeded stream. One `landing` line per region, every room in it one of the
region's members, and `set the Grounds's landing to the Hall` replaces the
whole list with that one room. A region with no landing cannot be moved
to, but `is in the Grounds` is a membership test either way: true in any
member room, with no `landing` line needed.
