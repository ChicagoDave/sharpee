Everything so far attaches behavior to things that already exist. The
`define` family is how you add to the language itself: name a condition,
name a bundle of text, teach the parser a verb, mint a trait or an
action other entities can be composed with — or alter the standard
library's grammar itself with `extend action` and `remove from action`.
Together with `create`, these are the language's top-level declarations,
and unlike `create` they declare *kinds and vocabulary*, not instances
in the world.
